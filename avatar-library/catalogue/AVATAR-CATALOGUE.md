## Compatibility levels

- **Source available:** a public repository or official download is identified.
- **Archive pinned:** a commit and expected hash are recorded in `../licensing-provenance/PROVENANCE.md`.
- **Import confirmed:** a package was accepted by an OpenSimulator instance. This alone does not prove the avatar is complete or wearable.
- **Viewer evaluated:** actual Aurora screenshots exist for front, side, face, standing and movement checks.
- **Portable:** the system was re-imported in a clean, independent OpenSimulator installation. Hypergrid portability is a separate untested claim.

Do not mark a candidate “ready” until all of the last three stages have evidence. A package being free to wear within another grid is not the same as a downloadable, importable or redistributable package.
